How estimated taxes work when income is uneven

When do I need to pay estimated tax?

Generally if you expect to owe at least $1,000 in federal tax after withholding and refundable credits, and your withholding and credits will be less than the smaller of 90% of this year's tax or 100% of last year's tax (110% of last year's if your prior-year AGI was above $150,000, or $75,000 if married filing separately). Source: IRS estimated tax FAQ.

What are the quarterly due dates?

For the 2026 tax year: April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15, 2027. Dates for 2027 generally follow the same pattern (April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15 of the next year); when a date lands on a weekend or holiday, the next business day applies. Check the current year's Form 1040-ES for exact dates. Source: IRS Form 1040-ES.

My income is lumpy. Do I still pay four equal amounts?

The IRS says taxpayers should generally pay in four equal amounts to avoid a penalty, but if you receive income unevenly you may be able to vary the payments using the annualized income installment method. Source: IRS Topic 306. The prior-year safe harbor in this calculator is the simplest way to get a predictable number.

How do I do this in Excel?

Put your safe-harbor target in one cell: =MIN(0.9*ExpectedTax, 1.0*PriorYearTax)-Withholding (use 1.1 instead of 1.0 for higher-income filers), then divide by 4 for each quarterly payment.
